GAIA

a deep time planet simulator

↑ four and a half billion years, in forty seconds

~ ~ ~

Gaia simulates the whole geological life of a planet — born as an ocean of molten rock, its first seas rained out of a steam sky, and from there six billion years of continents drifting, mountains rising and wearing back down, supercontinents assembling and shattering, life greening the land — until the engine inside finally cools, the magnetic field gutters out, and the world keeps only its scars.

Every world grows from a single seed, and every run is a story. Watch it unfold, hover anywhere to read the rocks beneath, nudge the sun or the mantle and see what happens, and when it's all over — rewind time and replay the entire life of your planet. Or branch history and try again.
